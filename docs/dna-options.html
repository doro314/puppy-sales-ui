Things to decide

  • Roll up or list all 9? Nine breeds with seven under 5% reads as noise on a phone. Bichon, Bolonka, Maltese and Bolognese are all close relatives, so a "Bichon family" group stays truthful and keeps the story at Poodle + Havanese.
  • Puppy or litter? This test belongs to one puppy. Littermates share parents but each gets a different mix, so showing it on the litter card or Home would claim more than the test does. A puppy-level field ("dna" on the puppy in litters.json) keeps it honest; testing the parents would be the way to say something about every litter.
  • The 2% Chihuahua. The roll-up puts it under "Other". If a buyer asks, the full list in Option B shows it, which is better than it surfacing later from the report.
  • Data shape, if it goes ahead: "dna": { "source": "Wisdom Panel", "breeds": [{ "breed": "Havanese", "pct": 37 }, …] }, with the grouping done in utils.js and the checker making sure the percentages add to 100.
