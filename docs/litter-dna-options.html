Things to decide

  • A and B work today with just Olive's test. Both name her, so nobody reads her mix as the whole litter's.
  • C is the strongest selling point but needs two kits. Once Coco and Leo are tested, their results carry to every litter they have, and the litter card stops depending on which puppies got tested.
  • The pieces combine: C on the litter card, A's badge on the tested puppies' cards, and the full breakdown on each puppy's own page (the earlier dna-options.html).
  • Data: nothing new at the litter level. A uses puppy.dna; C adds the same dna shape to mom and dad. The tile, the rows and the badge are all derived.
  • Small catch while I was in there: the vaccination note in litters.json reads "Rabbies-1yr". These mockups show it as "Rabies-1yr".
