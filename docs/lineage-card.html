Wider screens

The same card, open, on the real Home layout with the sidebar.

Desktop · 1280 px

From 1100 px the litter card sits beside the gallery in a column about 360 px wide, so the card looks just like it does on a phone. Nothing to change.

Tablet · 900 px

Between 768 and 1100 px the card runs the full content width. The breed list is capped at 420 px so the percentages stay next to their names; everything else just gets wider lines, which read fine.
